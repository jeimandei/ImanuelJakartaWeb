A Place for Everyone

No matter where you're coming from — whether you've been a Christian for decades, you're just beginning to explore faith, or you're somewhere in between — you will find a genuine welcome at Imanuel Jakarta Church.

We are a diverse, multigenerational community that worships together in both Indonesian and English. Our goal is simple: to know God deeply and help others do the same.

Come as you are. You don't need to have everything figured out. We're all on the journey together.

What to Expect

Here's what your first visit will look like

A Warm Welcome

Our friendly welcome team will greet you at the door, help you find your way, and answer any questions you have. No pressure, just warmth.

Uplifting Worship

Contemporary and traditional worship music that connects hearts to God. Lyrics are displayed so everyone can participate, regardless of background.

Relevant Messages

Biblical, practical teaching that connects Scripture to everyday life. Messages typically run 30–40 minutes and are available online after each service.

Kids' Programs

Age-appropriate programs for children during every Sunday service, so parents can worship freely while kids enjoy their own time with God.
