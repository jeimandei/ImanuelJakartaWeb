Why We Give

"On the first day of every week, each one of you should set aside a sum of money in keeping with your income, saving it up, so that when I come no collections will have to be made."

— 1 Corinthians 16:2

Giving is an act of worship and an expression of trust in God as our ultimate provider. At Imanuel Jakarta Church, we believe that everything we have belongs to God, and that generous giving is one of the most powerful ways we can participate in His Kingdom work.

Your tithes and offerings fund our local ministries, support missionaries, and help us serve the city of Jakarta and communities in need across Indonesia.

Local Ministries

Supporting programs and staff who serve our congregation

Missions

Partnering with missionaries locally and internationally

Community Outreach

Serving those in need in Jakarta and beyond

Facilities

Maintaining our building as a welcoming place to gather
