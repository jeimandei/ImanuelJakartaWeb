Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most often

We hold two Sunday worship services each week:
  • 08:00 WIB — First Service (Indonesian language)
  • 10:30 WIB — Second Service (English/Bilingual)
We also have a Wednesday Bible Study & Prayer at 19:00 WIB, a Thursday Youth Service at 19:00 WIB, and Friday Cell Groups at 19:00 WIB in various locations.

Yes! Free parking is available in our church compound for cars and motorcycles. We also have a parking team on duty every Sunday to help direct traffic and assist you in finding a spot. On busy Sundays, additional street parking is available on nearby roads.

Absolutely! Children are a vital part of our church family. We have age-appropriate children's programs running during both Sunday services for children from nursery (babies) through elementary school age. Our trained volunteers create a safe, fun, and faith-building environment for every child. Families with infants are also welcome to remain in the main sanctuary.

There is no dress code at Imanuel Jakarta Church. Come as you are! Most people dress in smart casual attire, but you will see everything from formal wear to jeans and a t-shirt. What matters most is that you come with an open heart — your clothes don't determine your welcome here.

There are many ways to get involved at Imanuel Jakarta:
  • Join a Cell Group for community and discipleship
  • Serve in one of our ministries (worship, children, youth, outreach, prayer, etc.)
  • Volunteer on the welcome team or audio-visual team
  • Participate in community outreach events
The best starting point is to attend a service and have a conversation with our welcome team or contact us directly.

Yes! We call them Cell Groups, and they meet every Friday at 19:00 WIB in homes and various locations across Jakarta. Cell Groups are small gatherings of 8–15 people who study Scripture, pray together, share life, and support one another. They are the heartbeat of our community life. Contact us to find a Cell Group near your area.

Church membership at GKJ Imanuel Jakarta is open to those who:
  • Have made a personal commitment to follow Jesus Christ
  • Have been baptised (or desire to be baptised)
  • Are committed to the life and mission of the church
We periodically offer a New Members' Class where you can learn more about our beliefs, vision, and what membership means. Please contact us or speak with one of our pastors to learn more.

Yes! Both Sunday services are streamed live on our YouTube channel every week. You can also watch past sermons and recordings on YouTube at any time. Visit our Livestream page to watch live, or browse our Sermons archive for previous messages.
