Senior Pastor

Rev. Dr. Imanuel Santoso has served as the Senior Pastor of GKJ Imanuel Jakarta for over fifteen years. He holds a Doctor of Ministry from a leading theological seminary and is passionate about biblical preaching, pastoral care, and equipping the congregation to live as faithful disciples in every sphere of life.

He and his wife, Maria, have three children and are deeply committed to building a church that reflects the love, justice, and truth of the Gospel.

Church Elders

Providing godly oversight, wisdom, and accountability for our congregation

Our Board of Elders is comprised of spiritually mature men and women who provide wisdom, governance, and pastoral accountability for our church. The elders meet monthly to pray for the congregation, discern God's direction, and ensure faithful stewardship of the church's ministry and resources.

A full listing of our current elders is available from the church office. We invite you to pray for our leadership as they faithfully serve the congregation.
